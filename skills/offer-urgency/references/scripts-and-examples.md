# Scripts and examples for urgency

Find the user's situation in the table, then use the matching section. Adapt names, dates and amounts to the user's offer.

| User's situation | Urgency type | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Sells year round, no natural start date | Pricing or bonus based | Year-round sales and price raises |
| Plans to raise the price | Pricing or bonus based | Year-round sales and price raises |
| Can start customers in groups | Rolling cohorts | Group starts |
| Local business that changes promotions often | Rolling seasonal | Dated seasonal promotions |
| The buyer's opportunity shrinks over time | Exploding opportunity | Opportunities that decay |
| Fears that a deadline will lose sales | Any | Answer to the fear of lost sales |

## Year-round sales and price raises

Close for a business that sells year round:

```
Let's get you started today so you keep the discount you came in for. We change our promotions about every four weeks, and I can't promise this one will be here next time.
```

Add "this one is better than most we have run" only if it is true.

Things that can expire while the core product stays for sale: a discount, a price level, or a bonus such as free installation, free onboarding or an extra workshop with a stated value.

The deadline belongs to the promotion, never to the service. A roofer who says he will not serve late buyers is lying; saying the promotion ends gives the same push.

Price raise announcement, sent to everyone in the pipeline before the new price takes effect:

```
On <date> the price goes from <current price> to <new price>. Anyone who signs up before then keeps the current price.
```

## Group starts

Close for a business that starts a new group every week, with or without a cap on group size:

```
If you sign up today, you start with the group that begins this Monday. If you wait, the next start is the Monday after.
```

Variant, for use only when a client really did drop out:

```
A client who joined a few weeks ago had to drop out, so there is an open place in the group that starts Monday. If you plan to do this at some point, starting now gets you results sooner for the same price.
```

Fewer start dates give a stronger push. Every other week works; twice a year is the strongest.

Reply for a buyer who arrives just after a group started, two options:

1. Offer a fast one-to-one onboarding as a bonus for signing today, and place them in the current group.
2. Preferred: tell them the next group starts soon, and that the wait gives them time to go through the materials and talk it over with their team or family. Offer a longer payment plan, which the later start makes possible.

## Dated seasonal promotions

Keep the core promotion the same and change its name and end date each period. Example sequence for a small service business:

- Back to School promotion, ends September 30
- Fall Tune-up promotion, ends October 31
- Black Friday promotion, ends November 30
- Year-End promotion, ends December 31

Use the last real day of each month. The dates go on the landing page and in every email and ad. When a period ends, swap in the next landing page and ad with the new name and dates.

## Opportunities that decay

- A price gap between two marketplaces that closes as more sellers find it.
- A new advertising platform before competitors arrive and raise costs.
- A job offer whose signing bonus drops each day the candidate waits.

Write one sentence saying why the opportunity shrinks, and name the cost of each week of delay.

## Answer to the fear of lost sales

In a week-long launch, the last 4 hours bring in up to 50 to 60 percent of sales, about 3 percent of the campaign's time. The buyers who act on the deadline outnumber the few who miss it, and most of those who miss it would not have bought without one.

## Examples for small software and service products

- Cohort course or group coaching: a new group starts every other Monday. The checkout page shows the next start date and says that buyers after Sunday night join the group two weeks later. A buyer who signs up the day after a start gets option 2 from "Group starts".
- Small paid SaaS tool or Chrome extension with a planned price raise: the launch price holds until a fixed date and then rises. The page and an email to the waitlist both state the date, and the price changes on that date. The tool stays for sale afterward, so the deadline applies to the price only.
- Freelance service: a free strategy session is included for clients who book before the end of the month, and the bonus is removed on the first of the next month.
